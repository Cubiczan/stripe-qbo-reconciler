Stripe → QuickBooks Payout Reconciler

Payout-level reconciliation that splits every Stripe payout into gross sales, fees, and refunds, proves it against the bank, and exports journal entries QuickBooks can import.

The problem

Ecommerce sellers routinely lose ~10 hours a month hand-matching Stripe payouts into QuickBooks — because a Stripe payout is a net number, but the books need the split. Book the net payout as revenue, the tempting shortcut, and revenue is understated while Stripe's processing costs never hit the P&L as an expense, so margins look better than they are and fee changes go unnoticed.

How it works

  • Group & verify — balance transactions are grouped by payout; every payout must foot: payout = gross − fees − refunds − chargebacks.
  • Split — each payout becomes booking lines for gross sales, Stripe fees, refunds, and chargebacks. A payout that doesn't foot gets an explicit suspense line, so the journal always balances and the gap can't hide.
  • Match — payouts are matched to bank deposits by exact amount within a 3-day window.
  • Exceptions, not silence — anything that doesn't foot, doesn't match a deposit, duplicates charges, or contains a chargeback is classified and queued for human review instead of being silently booked. Exports are QBO-ready JSON + CSV with debits equal to credits.
